◎ PHOSPHORORBIT

SID is tracking multiple targets in Earth orbit.

PhosphorOrbit transforms your iPhone into a Cold War-era space surveillance terminal. Track every catalogued object in Earth orbit — from the International Space Station to dead Soviet satellites and the growing cloud of debris threatening our future in space — all rendered on a retro CRT phosphor radar scope.

FEATURES

  • Real-time azimuth/elevation polar radar scope showing all satellites overhead
  • SGP4/SDP4 orbital propagation using official NORAD/CelesTrak data
  • Object classification: space stations, payloads, Starlink, rocket bodies, debris
  • Pass predictions with sky track diagrams — know exactly where to look
  • Live satellite tracking during active passes
  • Mercator ground track with day/night terminator
  • Orbital shell density histogram by altitude band
  • Kessler Syndrome dashboard: debris ratios, critical bands, avoidance manoeuvre rates
  • Offline resilience — cached TLE data keeps the scope running without connectivity
  • User watchlist for tracking specific objects
  • Green, amber, and white phosphor CRT colour themes
  • No accounts, no tracking, no clutter

DATA SOURCE

All orbital data is provided by CelesTrak, sourced from the US Space Force 18th Space Defense Squadron. No account or subscription required.
